Every ride recorded. Every horse looked after.

01Rides

Start the ride, put your phone away. The app does the counting.

Route, distance, speed and elevation keep recording with the screen locked. Gaits are recognised by combining GPS speed with the phone's motion sensor.

  • Training types: trail, dressage, flatwork, jumping, cross-country, poles, lunging
  • Photos, a rating and a note for every ride
  • Ride history and statistics: distance, time in the saddle, gait breakdown
  • Saved to Apple Health as an equestrian workout — if you want
02Horse

Passport, health and feeding — at hand, even at the vet.

The horse card gathers what usually ends up scattered across notebooks: passport details, visit history and documents. Reminders speak up before vaccinations, the farrier or deworming.

  • Health history: vaccinations, farrier, deworming, physiotherapy — with costs
  • The horse's documents and photos in one place
  • A feeding plan with portions from your own feed store
  • Upkeep costs, one-off and recurring, with a monthly summary
  • Leases with attendance days, and mare pregnancy
03Stable

One board for the whole yard.

The owner creates the stable, everyone else joins with a code. No more notes on the tack-room door, no more booking the indoor arena across several group chats.

  • Tasks for staff, ticked off with who did them and when
  • Bookings for the arena, outdoor school and indoor hall — with a warning when slots overlap
  • Stable chat and roles: owner, staff, liveries
04Community

Exercises, a forum and the people you ride with.

An exercise library by training type, a forum for ideas and questions, groups with chat. Share your horse with a friend, and let your trainer plan a session that appears in your calendar.

  • Exercise library from basic to advanced
  • Forum, groups, 1:1 chat and friends
  • Horse sharing, and friends' rides on your horse
  • Trainer panel: students and planned sessions
  • Trips: trail rides, competitions and clinics — with a participant list
